How it works

Editing, writing and design work takes time and skill. Let me take that work off your hands.

Sorting through photos in a way that best tells the story is an art. So is writing just the right amount of copy. Don't end up with a jumble. Make your book the best it can be.

4 easysteps
1

Say hello

Book a free consultation by phone, video, or in person so we can talk through your project, timing and the kind of book you want to create.

2

Choose your photos

Search your phone or computer, select favorites, and gather them into one folder. You can share digital images or give me prints to scan. Every print is returned promptly.

Photo sharing made simple

The easiest workflow is to collect your images in one Google Drive folder and share that folder with Storyline. If you would rather upload directly, there is space for that too.

How to prepare your photos

  1. 1

    Search your camera roll, desktop folders, text threads, and cloud albums for the pictures that belong in your project.

  2. 2

    Create a folder named for your book, like Smith Anniversary Book.

  3. 3

    Drop your favorites into that folder.

    • Rule of thumb: 150–350 photos per book (maximum 500)
    • It's fine if there are more photos than are used
    • Name photos by location or subject — e.g. Tokyo, Osaka — not default JPEG names
  4. 4

    Upload the folder to Google Drive.

  5. 5

    Share access with Storyline.

Please bring or ship photos roughly organized (by year, event, or family member) when possible. If photos arrive unsorted, a sorting and curation fee of $45/hour applies so I can group them into chapters and a clear storyline.

If you are unsure how to organize things, send what you have and give us a call. Storyline can help shape the photo selection from there.

Scanning & Sorting Photos

Turning boxes and albums into a beautiful Storyline book starts with careful, high-quality scanning and thoughtful sorting. Every print is handled as an irreplaceable memory.

Standard Photo Scanning (600 DPI)

Loose, clean 3x5, 4x6, or 5x7 prints that can be safely fed through a photo scanner.

Rate: $0.40 per photo.

Premium Flatbed Scanning

For fragile, torn, Polaroid, odd sizes, or prints stuck to pages that need extra care and higher resolution for enlargement.

Rate: $2.50 per photo.

Album Handling

Careful removal from old “magnetic” albums or manual scanning of full pages when removal isn't safe.

Rate: $45 per hour.

Back-of-Photo Notes

Dates, names, and captions on the backs of prints can be scanned and used as inspiration for the text in your book.

Rate: $0.10 per photo.

Sorting & Curation

If your photos arrive already grouped by decade, event, or family branch, scanning goes faster and stays in order. If you prefer to hand over a “shoebox of everything,” I can sort and group them for you.

Sorting fee: $45 per hour.

Pricing basics

Clear and honest pricing

Storyline services

$4.50 per page with 20-page minimum plus tax, plus charges for photo book vendors (see below).

Printing & vendor costs

Printing, tax and shipping are paid to the photo book vendor (Shutterfly, Mixbook, Vistaprint, etc.). Storyline researches and recommends the best vendor for the project. Pricing varies by size, pages and specs. Vendor discounts are applied.

When payment is due

Payment is due upon placement of order, based on the estimated number of pages and book specs. An estimate is supplied before work begins, and adjustments can be made if more pages are added.

Accepted payments

ACH, Venmo, cash and Zelle preferred. We also accept Visa and Mastercard.

Base Storyline Book Package includes custom layout and design plus scanning of up to 40 photos at 600 DPI. Additional physical photos are billed at the standard or premium scan rates above. Minimum scanning fee: $30 per project to cover setup and digital organization.
